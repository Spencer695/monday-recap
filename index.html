How to get the file from Shopify

  1. In your Shopify admin, open Orders and click Export.
  2. Choose a date range that covers at least the last two weeks. A few months gives better new customer counts.
  3. Pick CSV for Excel, Numbers, or other spreadsheet programs and export.
  4. Load the file here with the button above, or drag it onto the page.
How the numbers are calculated
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday, using the order dates in your export.
  • Revenue is the order total, including shipping and tax, minus refunds. Refunds count in the week the order was placed.
  • Canceled and voided orders are left out of every number.
  • A new customer is someone whose first order in your file falls in that week, matched by email. Orders without an email count toward sales only.
  • Product sales use item price times quantity, before order discounts.
